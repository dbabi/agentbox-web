~/snib

Snib rhymes with crib. it is a small lockbox with feet.

its name is a Scots word for the little catch that bolts a door.

Snib gives every agent task a sealed room of its own.

the gold padlock rides outside the box, because the keys never go in.

before an agent does anything risky, Snib waits for its human to sign.

the story follows the design. most of that design is not built yet.

poses

  • happy: the default.
  • thinking: work in progress, planning, loading.
  • guarding: security pages and approval prompts.
  • sleeping: idle, standby and empty states.

the idle loop: 4 frames of 500 ms. press m to pause or play.
